# Code of conduct

This is a small project with a small number of people around it, so this is
short.

Be civil. Argue about the code, not about the person who wrote it. Assume the
other person is trying to help until they give you a reason to think otherwise.
Harassment, personal attacks and unwanted contact are not welcome here, in the
issues, in pull requests or anywhere else the project reaches.

If someone is making the project a worse place to be, open a
[private report](https://github.com/Talkdedsec/tlk-visual/security/advisories/new)
or contact [@Talkdedsec](https://github.com/Talkdedsec) directly. Reports stay
private. Depending on what happened, the answer can be a word in private, an
edited or removed comment, or a block.

The maintainer has the last word on all of it.
